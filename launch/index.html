A

What's usually missing, and what we keep

An app made in an app builder can look right and work well for the person who built it. Once real people sign up, it often lacks the basics: who can see whose data, what happens when something fails, backups, tests, and code the next developer can pick up. Section C lists what we deliver for each of them.

Where a part has to be rebuilt, the scope says which part and why, and whether you can keep editing the app in your app builder afterward, so you know before you pay.

B

What's guaranteed

You pay half when you accept the scope, and the other half only once every acceptance check in it passes on a private demo of your app. If the checks aren't all passing within 10 business days of the date in your scope, for reasons that aren't yours, you can cancel and get back everything you've paid. If we stop the build before you've accepted it, for a reason that isn't yours, the same full refund applies.

  • The checks come first. They're written into your scope before any work starts, and each is something you can do or see yourself on the demo.
  • If it's late, you choose. The date in your scope moves only for delays on your side, such as an answer or material arriving after it's due, or for a change to the scope that we both accept. Once the 10 business days have passed, you can cancel and have everything you've paid refunded, or take the work as it stands for the first half, with nothing more to pay.
  • If you don't reply. You have 5 business days to try the checks. If we hear nothing, we send a reminder naming the date the work will count as accepted, at least 5 business days later.
  • A failing check costs you nothing extra. Fixing it is part of the fixed price, and it doesn't use up your round of changes.
  • Defects fixed at no charge for 30 days. The fix window starts when we transfer the code to you after you've accepted the work, and your scope can make it longer. If something doesn't do what your scope or an acceptance check says and you report it in writing during the window, we fix it at no charge. That includes an app that passes its checks on the demo but fails a check in your accounts after it's put live as our guide says. It doesn't cover later changes to the code or settings, or problems at a hosting provider or another service. If we stop during the window with a defect you've reported still open, we refund 10 percent of what you paid.
  • Correction or refund. If a deliverable doesn't match the signed scope and you tell us in writing within 30 days of delivery on the demo, we correct it, and if we don't correct it within a reasonable time, we refund the part of the fee that relates to it. If an acceptance check never passes, the second half isn't charged, and the terms set out the rest.

Each of these is in your written scope or in the terms it includes.

C

What "production ready" means here

Each of these is delivered, and the handover document shows the evidence.

  • Tests for everything you'll check. Every acceptance check in your scope has an automated test that does what the check describes, in a real browser. The handover says which test covers which check.
  • Tests on every change. They run on their own each time the code changes, and a change that fails them is flagged before it goes live.
  • Security checks. Each kind of user can see and change only what the scope says, tried against the server directly as well as through the screens. No passwords or keys in the code. Every library the app builds on is checked for known security problems. Forms refuse bad input, the app is served over HTTPS only, and repeated failed sign-ins are slowed down.
  • Errors handled and logged. When something fails, people see a plain message instead of a crash page. The error is logged with enough detail to find the cause, and without passwords or card details.
  • Backups that work. Automatic database backups are part of the setup, and we restore one on the demo before delivery to show it works.
  • A deployment you own. Once the code is yours, you put the app live in accounts in your name, with you as the top administrator, following our written step-by-step guide. You type every password and key yourself, and we never ask for them. We can also set up the hosting account for you, as an add-on under "Putting it live" below, and at the hand-off you replace the first password we set.
  • A handover document. How the app fits together, how to run the tests and ship a change, every account it uses and what each costs, how to restore a backup, and what to do if it stops working.
  • A walkthrough. We walk you through every acceptance check on the demo, recorded for you to keep.

The security checks are part of our own delivery checklist. They aren't a security audit, a penetration test or a certification.

D

How it works

It starts with an email, and there's no sales call to book. We work with businesses only.

  1. Email us. Send a link to the prototype, or a short description of it and what it was built with, to hello@cedarwood.studio. Please don't send passwords or real customer records at this stage.
  2. A look and a few questions. We look at the prototype before quoting, without changing anything, and if the quote needs the code, we ask for read-only access. Then we reply with a few questions, none of them technical, each with what we'll assume if you're not sure, so "fine" is a complete answer. Ask us anything too, about what to keep, what to change or how the work runs. Until you accept the scope, there's no charge for this.
  3. A written scope with a fixed price. What you'll get, what's kept and what's rebuilt, the checks you'll try yourself to accept it, the delivery date and the price. You accept it by email, and from then on it's fixed. A change beyond the one round of changes below is priced in writing before any work.
  4. The work. You pay the first half by card through Stripe when you accept the scope. We work from a copy of your app's code, exported from the app builder or read from its repository, and run it on a private demo with made-up data. We don't need your app builder account or your live data.
  5. Delivery and a walkthrough. We walk you through every check on the demo, and you try them yourself. One round of changes is included. The second half is due once every check passes and the work is accepted.
  6. The handover. We transfer the code to a repository you own, and you put the app live in accounts in your name with our step-by-step guide and our help by message. The fix window starts that day.
