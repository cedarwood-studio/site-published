Terms

Who you are dealing with

The agreement and what it consists of

No work starts until both of us have signed a written scope, or accepted it by email, that states the service, the deliverables, any gate, the timeline and the price, and that refers to these terms. In these terms, a scope accepted by email counts as signed. The scope and these terms together are the agreement for that service, which these terms call an engagement. Each service is a separate engagement.

If the documents disagree, they apply in this order:

  1. the signed scope or proposal;
  2. these terms;
  3. the pages of this website.

The pages of this website describe the services in general. They are descriptions and are not warranties. A statement on a page binds us only if the scope repeats it or these terms do. The scope lists the deliverables for the service. Before you sign, check that it repeats what matters to you. The sections of these terms on gates, stopping work, warranty and liability apply whatever the scope says, unless the scope names the section it changes.

In these terms, work starts on the first day on which the fee due before work starts is paid and we have the access the scope calls for. Delivery is the day we send you the deliverables the scope lists or, for a service with a gate, the day the gate is met. The timeline is the working period the scope states, counted in business days from the day work starts. Where the scope states a delivery date, the timeline ends on that date.

The version of these terms posted on the date you sign the scope applies to that engagement. A later version applies only to a scope signed after it is posted. A change to a signed scope is made in writing and accepted by both of us before the changed work is done.

Payment

You enter your card details on Stripe's pages, and we never see or store your full card number. Invoices are due within 14 days of the invoice date. If an invoice is overdue, we may pause work until it is paid.

Unless the scope says otherwise:

  • A service priced above $5,000 is paid in two equal installments. The first is invoiced when the scope is signed, and work does not start until it is paid. The second becomes due only when the gate named in the scope is met.

Where an offer says a fee is credited against a later service, the credit applies only to the service named, and only if you ask us in writing for that service within the period the offer states, counted from delivery of the earlier service, and a scope for it is then signed. If the offer states no period, the credit has no deadline. We are not obliged to take on the later service. A credit reduces the first installment of the later service. The credit itself has no cash value and is not refunded, and where these terms refund part of an installment, the same part of the money you actually paid toward it is refunded. For the limit of liability below, an applied credit counts as fees paid for the later service.

Gates and fix windows

A gate is an objective check written in the scope, such as a pipeline passing on three consecutive pushes or a scheduled check meeting an agreed threshold. A gate is met when that check first passes as the scope describes it.

A gate is a condition for a payment. It is not a promise, a guarantee or a warranty that the result will be reached. We do not promise that any gate will be met, and a gate that is not met is not a breach of the agreement. The consequence of a gate not being met is that the installment tied to it is not charged.

If the work is ready for the gate and you prevent the check from being run, for example by withdrawing access, and that continues for 10 business days after we tell you in writing, the gate is treated as met. The same applies if you cancel after we have told you in writing that the work is ready for the gate.

A fix window is a period after delivery in which we correct defects at no charge. A service has a fix window only where its offer page or its scope names one. Unless the scope says otherwise, it starts on the day the gate is met, or on the day of delivery where there is no gate, and runs for 30 calendar days. It covers a defect in the work we delivered, measured against the signed scope and against any check the scope names, that you report to us in writing within the window. It does not cover the effects of changes made by you or by anyone else, changes in the systems, data or third-party services the work connects to, or new requirements. The section "If we stop work" applies to a fix window.

Refunds

  • Refunds on cancellation and on a stop are set out in the next two sections.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of the refund falling due.

If you cancel

  • Either of us may cancel an engagement before work starts at no cost, and any prepayment is refunded in full.
  • You may cancel an engagement after work starts by telling us in writing.
  • If the service was paid in full before work started, we refund the fee in proportion to the part of the scope's timeline that had not yet run on the day we received your notice, counted in business days from the day work started. We send you the work as it stands. If we have not delivered within 10 business days after the end of the timeline, for reasons that are not yours, you may cancel for a full refund.
  • If the service is paid in two installments, we keep the first installment in proportion to how much of the first half of the timeline had run on the day we received your notice, and refund the rest. Once half the timeline has run, we keep all of it. The second installment is not charged, unless the gate was met before your notice or is treated as met under the section above. We hand over the work as it stands.
  • Ongoing arrangements, where the scope provides for one, can be ended by you with 14 days' written notice. We may end one under the next section, and fees prepaid for the time after it ends are refunded.

If we stop work

We may stop work on any engagement, and on any fix window, at any time and for any reason, by telling you in writing. We have no obligation to continue an engagement, to reach a gate or to complete a fix. Stopping under this section is not a breach of the agreement.

If we stop:

  • We charge no further fees for that engagement. An installment that is not yet due is not charged, and an installment tied to a gate is not charged unless the gate was met before we stopped.
  • Where the service was paid in full before work started and we stop before delivering it, we refund the fee in full.
  • Where the service is paid in two installments, we hand over the work in the state it is in. If we stop within five business days after work starts, we refund the first installment in full. If we stop later, we keep the first installment in proportion to how much of the first half of the timeline had run on the day we stopped, as payment in full for the work up to the stop, and refund the rest. Once half the timeline has run, we keep all of it.
  • Where we stop during a fix window while a defect the window covers, reported to us in writing, is still open, we refund 10 percent of the amount you paid for that service.
  • We tell you in writing what is finished and what was in progress, we list any setting we changed outside a branch, we leave work in progress on a branch separate from your main branch, and we stop using the access you gave us.

The items above are your only remedies if we stop work, if a gate is not met, or if a defect is not fixed in a fix window. In those cases you have no claim against us for completion of the work, for the cost of having someone else complete it, or for damages arising from the work not being completed.

Access and deletion

We ask for the least access the work needs, read-only where possible. We never ask for write access to your production systems or for the credentials those systems run on.
