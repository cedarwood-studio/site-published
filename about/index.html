About the studio

Cedarwood Studio is a one-person software and infrastructure studio in Novato, California. It's the business name of Jack Senechal, and he's accountable for everything it delivers.

Last updated November 17, 2026

A

Jack Senechal

Jack was an engineering manager and principal software engineer at Mavenlink (now Kantata) from 2017 to 2023. He inherited a one-person DevOps team after mass departures and rebuilt it, and over several years led the move of the company's Rails monolith and its services onto Kubernetes. He designed and operated CI/CD pipelines there.

Before that, at the same company, he took over its integrations platform when it was unreliable and cut its error rate roughly 100x. He then moved that platform from Heroku to Google Cloud with a zero-downtime cutover.

In 2024, as an infrastructure engineer at Tobiko, he built the infrastructure for the company's hosted product on Google Cloud and built and maintained its CI/CD pipelines. He has a BA in mathematics from the University of North Carolina at Asheville.

That record comes from his years inside companies. The studio itself is new and has no client work to show yet, so the proof it offers is the public samples below.

B

How the work is done

The work is done with AI tooling and automated verification. The AI tool is Anthropic's Claude, with model training on our data turned off. Reports are produced inside a pipeline of automated checks. For an Upgrade Roadmap, a review pass that had no part in writing the report goes through it first.

A person reviews each deliverable before it goes out. Jack answers for the result.

Each report comes with the data and the queries or checks behind its numbers, so you can verify it yourself.

C

How your code is handled

  • We ask for the least access the work needs, read-only where possible, and never for production credentials.
  • For an Upgrade Roadmap, any code we run goes in a container that holds no credentials.
  • The code and materials you give us access to are sent, in whole or in part, to Anthropic's Claude to do the work. Model training on our data is turned off.
  • We delete our copies when you ask.
  • What passes through Anthropic is held there under its retention policy. We don't control that, and we can't delete it.

The terms have the full version, under how we handle your code and data.
