Gates and fix windows

A gate is an objective check written in the scope, such as a pipeline passing on three consecutive pushes or a scheduled check meeting an agreed threshold. A gate is met when that check first passes as the scope describes it.

A gate is a condition for a payment. It is not a promise, a guarantee or a warranty that the result will be reached. We do not promise that any gate will be met, and a gate that is not met is not a breach of the agreement. The consequence of a gate not being met is that the installment tied to it is not charged.

If the work is ready for the gate and you prevent the check from being run, for example by withdrawing access, and that continues for 10 business days after we tell you in writing, the gate is treated as met. The same applies if you cancel after we have told you in writing that the work is ready for the gate. For a build, this paragraph does not apply, and "Builds" sets out instead when the work is treated as accepted.

A fix window is a period after delivery in which we correct defects at no charge. A service has a fix window only where its offer page or its scope names one. Unless the scope says otherwise, it starts on the day the gate is met, or on the day of delivery where there is no gate, and runs for 30 calendar days. For a build, it starts instead on the day we transfer the repository after acceptance, as set out under "Builds". It covers a defect in the work we delivered, measured against the signed scope and against any check the scope names, that you report to us in writing within the window. It does not cover the effects of changes made by you or by anyone else, changes in the systems, data or third-party services the work connects to, or new requirements. The section "If we stop work" applies to a fix window.

Builds

A build is an engagement in which we make a new app, or finish, fix or rebuild an existing one, to a written scope at a fixed price, and hand it over to you as a code repository. The scope says when an engagement is a build. Reviews, reports and diagnostics, such as the Upgrade Roadmap and the CI Diagnostic, are not builds, and this section does not apply to them. For a build, this section applies in place of the parts of other sections that it names. Where it says nothing, the rest of these terms apply. In this section, business days leave out weekends and US federal holidays.
