D

How it works

It starts with an email, and there's no sales call to book.

  1. Email us a few lines on what the app is for and who will use it.
  2. Questions both ways. We reply with a few questions, none of them technical, and answer yours by email, at no charge before you accept a scope.
  3. A written scope with a fixed price. The features in your words, the checks you'll try yourself, the delivery date and the price. You accept it by email and pay the first half by card.
  4. The build and the handover. We build it, run it on a private demo with made-up data and walk you through every check, with one round of changes included. Once the work is accepted, you pay the second half and the code moves to a repository you own.
