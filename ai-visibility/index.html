A

What you get

  • A report on what the assistants say. The first page says how many of your customers' questions get an answer that names you, who gets named instead and from which of their pages, and any wrong facts about you, such as old hours, a wrong phone number or a service you don't offer.
  • Fixes, ranked by the evidence behind them. Each fix says how strong that evidence is, from documented by the assistants' own makers down to unproven. They're split into changes to your website and the steps that are yours to take, like your Google Business Profile, Yelp and reviews. Much of what's sold as AI SEO has no published evidence behind it. Google says there are "no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary" (Google Search Central). The report says so for any popular fix that has none.
  • Every answer, word for word. The assistants' full answers come at the back, so you can read for yourself what they said.
  • A check that the assistants can reach and read your site, and that the facts about you match across your website and listings.
  • A recorded walkthrough of your report, to watch when it suits you.

The report comes as a designed PDF, and as a web page at a private address if you'd like one. There's nothing to log into.

B

How we measure

  • We work out the questions with you, then fix them. After your form, we email you a first draft of 15 to 20 questions in your customers' words, drawn from your answers and from what customers of businesses like yours tend to ask, each with a line on why it's there. You add, cut and reword until the list sounds like your customers. Take as many emails as it needs; we expect most lists to settle within a few days. When the list is right, we send it to you as the final list, with the assistants and the delivery date. Once you reply OK to that email, the list stays as it is for that measurement, so the result can't be tuned after the fact.
  • Each one is asked several times. The same question asked twice often gets a different answer. In one published test of local home-service questions, a second run kept 79% of the businesses named the first time (Doser, October 2026). The report shows you that wobble, so one lucky or unlucky answer doesn't count for more than it should.
  • Some by hand, too. The apps can answer differently from one person to the next, since they know things like your location. So we also ask a few of your questions in the ChatGPT app and the Gemini app, and report those apart. Under a Fix or a Re-check we ask them again.

The Baseline changes nothing on your site. Nobody can promise that an assistant will name or rank a business, and we don't. The report also says how to tell whether calls and bookings change.

How it works

There's no call to book. We work with businesses only, and the terms apply.

Businesses whose customers find them by asking who to hire: local services, and firms that sell their expertise.

Your data
