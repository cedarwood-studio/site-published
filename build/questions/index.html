Custom web app: questions buyers ask

Short answers about a new web app built by Cedarwood Studio, from the price to what happens after handover. The offer itself is on the custom web app page, and the full rules are in the terms.

Last updated November 28, 2026

Price and payment

How much does a custom web app cost?

From $2,500, at a fixed price you have in writing before you pay anything. We quote it from your answers to a few questions, and it depends on what the app has to do and what it connects to, such as sign-in, payments or email. Hosting and other services are billed to you by their providers, and your scope estimates them.

When do I pay?

Half when you accept the written scope, by card through Stripe, and the other half when the work is accepted, after every acceptance check in your scope passes on the demo. Nothing is charged until you accept the scope.

Can the price go up during the build?

Only if you agree to it. The price and the scope change only through a change order that you accept in writing, and the date moves only for that or for delays on your side. One round of changes after the walkthrough is part of the fixed price.

If something goes wrong

What happens if the build is late?

If the checks aren't all passing 10 business days after the date in your scope, for reasons that aren't yours, you can cancel. You then choose between getting back everything you've paid and taking the work as it stands for the first half, with nothing more to pay and no fix window. The date moves only for delays on your side, such as an answer arriving after it's due, or for a change order you accept.

What if a check fails on the demo?

We fix it, and that's part of the fixed price. A failing check doesn't use up your round of changes, and the second half isn't due until every check passes.

What if I'm slow to try the checks?

You get 5 business days to try them. If we hear nothing, we send you a reminder before the work counts as accepted, and the terms give the dates. A check that fails later is still covered by the fix window.

What if something breaks after handover?

For 30 days after we transfer the code to you, we fix at no charge anything that doesn't do what your scope or an acceptance check says, if you report it in writing. That includes the app failing a check in your accounts after you put it live as our guide says. It doesn't cover later changes to the code or settings, or problems at a hosting provider or another service.

Can I cancel?

Yes, in writing at any time. Before work starts, you get a full refund. During the build we keep a share of the first half in proportion to how much of the first half of the timeline has run, and all of it once half the timeline has run, and you get the work as it stands. Once the app is delivered, canceling without reporting a failed check counts as accepting it. The terms have the details.

Getting started

What should I send in the first email?

A few lines on what the app is for and who will use it. The button on the offer page opens an email that lists the rest: the three to five things someone must be able to do with it, anything it connects to, and a date that matters. Please don't send real customer records at this stage.

Is there a sales call?

No. Everything up to the scope happens by email, and we answer your questions there, at no charge before you accept a scope. The walkthrough at delivery is recorded for you to keep.

How long does a build take?

The delivery date is in your written scope, set from what the app has to do, before you pay anything.

Who do you build for?

Businesses that need a web app for their customers, their staff or the public, used in a browser on a computer or a phone. We work with businesses only.

Can you build it in another language or on my hosting?

Often, yes. By default we build in TypeScript on Cloudflare. If you need a different language, framework or host, say so in your first email, and we'll tell you whether we can and at what price before you commit. What it's built on.

Who owns the app?

You do, once it's paid for: the code, the repository and the documents, with open-source parts under their own licenses. The app runs in accounts in your name.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. We can sign a mutual NDA before you send anything, as long as it allows the AI processing our terms describe.
