Privacy

This site sets no cookies. It counts visits with a counter that stores nothing in your browser. Its one form is the intake form a buyer of the AI Visibility Baseline fills in after paying, and otherwise the only way to reach us is an email.

Last updated November 28, 2026

Privacy

Cookies and visit counting

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

We count visits with PostHog, hosted in the EU. To count a visitor without a cookie, PostHog combines your IP address and browser details into a code that changes every day, and it doesn't keep the IP address. It records the page, the site that sent you, the campaign tag in the link, your browser (with its operating system, screen size, language and time zone), your country, how far you scrolled before you left, and whether you clicked a sample report, clicked the email button or copied our email address or subject. It never receives your name, your email address or anything you send us.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the counter doesn't load. To opt out, turn either one on in your browser. We don't use advertising pixels, and we don't share visit data with ad platforms.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS and forwards email sent to hello@cedarwood.studio. Like any web host, GitHub processes standard request data, such as your IP address, to serve the site. The pages load their fonts, scripts and images from this site. The visit counter loads through our own address, e.cedarwood.studio, which passes requests on to PostHog.

Email

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write. We keep the message so we can reply and as a business record, in a mailbox run by our email provider. Our service providers process what you send us, including our email provider and Anthropic, whose AI model, Claude, we work with. The terms say how AI tools handle it. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell what you send or share it for advertising. If you arrive from one of our ads, the email button adds a short reference to the subject line. You can delete it.

The intake form

After you buy an AI Visibility Baseline, a form asks about your business and your customers. We receive your answers, the email address you give, your name if you give it, and the reference of your Stripe checkout, so we can match your answers to your payment. The form sends them to a service of ours hosted by Cloudflare, which stores them and emails a copy to us through our email provider. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request data, such as your IP address, and our service doesn't store it. The form sets no cookies.

We use your answers to do the work and keep them as a business record. Our service providers process what you send, including our email provider and Anthropic, whose AI model, Claude, we work with. The questions we agree with you, which name your business and its area, are sent to the assistants being measured, as the terms describe under "AI Visibility services". The stored copy at Cloudflare is deleted automatically 180 days after you send it, and we delete our other copies when you ask, apart from the records we keep for tax and legal purposes. Copies left in the backups of our customer records age out within 30 days of the deletion. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell your answers or share them for advertising.

Payments

Payment is by card through Stripe, from a payment link on an offer page, or a payment link or invoice we send by email. You enter your card details on Stripe's pages, and Stripe handles them under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card number. We do see the record of the payment, which includes your name, your email address, the amount and whether it was paid, and we keep it as a business record.

Client materials

Code, documents and access you give us during an engagement aren't collected through this site. They're covered by the written scope and by the section of the terms on how we handle your code and data.
