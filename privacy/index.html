Privacy

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It runs no analytics and no tracking scripts.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS and forwards email sent to hello@cedarwood.studio. Like any web host, GitHub processes standard request data, such as your IP address, to serve the site. The pages load nothing from any other host: no analytics, fonts, scripts or images from third parties.

Email

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write. We keep the message so we can reply and as a business record, in a mailbox run by our email provider. Our service providers, including our email provider and AI tooling providers, process what you send us. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell what you send or share it for advertising.

Payments

Payment is by card through Stripe, from a payment link or invoice we send by email. You enter your card details on Stripe's pages, and Stripe handles them under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card number. We do see the record of the payment, which includes your name, your email address, the amount and whether it was paid, and we keep it as a business record.

Client materials

Code, documents and access you give us during an engagement aren't collected through this site. They're covered by the written scope and by the section of the terms on how we handle your code and data.
